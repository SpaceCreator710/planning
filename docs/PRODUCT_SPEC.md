# AI Plan Your Day — Product specification

## Promise

Turn a chaotic day into one readable chain, protect what is fixed, and keep the next action realistic when time or capacity changes.

## Main tabs

1. **Today:** colorful connected timeline, free-time windows, drag reorder, focus and Reality Replan.
2. **Calendar:** day/week/month/year views, built-in events and Apple Calendar/Reminders connection.
3. **Health:** sleep, steps, activity, standing, Capacity Twin and workout timer.
4. **Notes:** internal notes, search, Inbox conversion and system sharing.
5. **Coach:** Soft, Strict and controlled Aggressive modes with task actions.
6. **Me:** profile, goals, progress, palette, integrations, AI connection and subscription.

## Core systems

- **Day Chain:** connected category-color capsules with SF Symbols, task details and accessible status controls.
- **Reality Engine:** reschedules unfinished work after delay, lower capacity or a new event while preserving completed/protected items.
- **Capacity Twin:** combines planned load, user signals and optional local wellness summaries into a conservative capacity indicator.
- **Collision Radar:** finds schedule overlap, important dates and overload before AI proposes changes.
- **Plan DNA:** learns useful time windows and block length from actual behavior.

## Platform commitments

- Responsive iPhone, iPad, Android and Web interface.
- Apple Calendar, Reminders, HealthKit, widgets and Share Extension on iOS development/release builds.
- Built-in calendar, notes, health log, timer and `.ics` portability on every platform.
- Full Apple Notes database synchronization is excluded because there is no public system API for it.

## Design commitments

- System typography and SF Symbols on Apple platforms.
- Rounded cards and pills with spring motion and a moving selected indicator.
- Ten accent palettes, six full-app canvases and independent task colors.
- Original hierarchy and components rather than reproducing competitor artwork or exact layouts.

## Business model

- Free: complete daily planner, built-in calendar/notes/health, basic AI and customization.
- Plus: recurrence, native Apple integrations, widgets, week planning and Strict mode.
- Pro: Aggressive mode, Collision Radar, advanced analytics and month strategy.
- Lifetime: $129.99 once, all Pro features and year planning.

Production billing requires store-verified entitlements and localized price retrieval.
