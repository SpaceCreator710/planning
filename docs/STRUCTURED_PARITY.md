## Original product advantages

- **Capacity Twin:** combines schedule load with optional local wellness summaries and suggests a realistic workload. Health data is not treated as a diagnosis.
- **Collision Radar:** detects calendar conflicts, overloaded days and important dates before AI moves unfinished work.
- **Reality Engine:** repairs only the unfinished portion of a day after a user signals delay, low capacity or an unexpected event.
- **Plan DNA:** learns useful work windows and block sizes from actual completion patterns.
- Three deliberately different coaching tones and a single behavior memory shared by day, week, month and year planning.

The design borrows platform conventions—SF Pro through system typography, SF Symbols, rounded geometry and spring motion—while keeping an original information hierarchy and color system.
