# Subscription design

Displayed catalog pricing is intentionally below a roughly $30/year visual-planner competitor at the Plus and Pro annual levels. Production must use localized prices returned by the store SDK.

| Capability | Free | Plus — $2.99/mo or $17.99/yr | Pro — $5.99/mo or $26.99/yr | Lifetime — $129.99 once |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Plans / coach / rescues per day | 2 / 4 / 2 | 6 / 10 / 6 | 15 / 30 / 15 | 40 / 100 / 40 |
| Planning horizon | Day | Week | Month | Year |
| Coach modes | Soft | Soft + Strict | All three | All three |
| Visual chain, calendar, notes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Built-in health + workout timer | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| `.ics` import/export | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Recurring routines | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Apple Calendar + Reminders | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Apple Health + Capacity Twin | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| iOS widgets + richer alerts | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Collision Radar + analytics | — | — | Yes | Yes |
| Deep behavioral memory | — | Basic | Advanced | Long-term |
| Future core upgrades | — | During subscription | During subscription | Included |

Why this split:

- Free is useful on day one and is not a crippled trial.
- Plus contains the paid competitor categories at a lower annual price: device sync, recurrence, widgets and stronger AI.
- Pro sells the original adaptive intelligence rather than arbitrary color or interface locks.
- Lifetime is above $100 as requested and avoids pretending to be a recurring subscription.

`Aggressive` means controlled, short and action-first. It must never insult, shame, threaten, target identity or encourage unhealthy behavior.
